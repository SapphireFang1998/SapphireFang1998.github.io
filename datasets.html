Datasets

Below are the datasets I have created and published for research purposes. All datasets are freely available for academic use.

Multilingual Sentiment Analysis Dataset

Published in 2023

A comprehensive dataset for sentiment analysis in 10 different languages. The dataset contains labeled reviews from various domains including movies, products, and restaurants.

Size: 2.3 GB, 1.5M samples

Related Papers:

  • Efficient Data Augmentation Techniques for Low-Resource Languages
Access Dataset

Medical Image Classification Dataset

Published in 2022

A curated collection of medical images for classification tasks. The dataset includes X-rays, MRIs, and CT scans with expert annotations for various medical conditions.

Size: 5.7 GB, 50K images

Related Papers:

  • Towards Explainable AI: A Case Study in Medical Diagnosis
Access Dataset

Urban Sound Classification Dataset

Published in 2021

A dataset of urban sounds for environmental sound classification. The dataset contains recordings from various urban environments labeled with sound categories.

Size: 1.8 GB, 8K audio clips
